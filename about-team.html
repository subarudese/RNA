About Us

Overview

Our laboratory, affiliated with the School of Biological Engineering at Dalian University of Technology, was established in 2019. It primarily focuses on research in nucleic acid vaccines and therapeutics, nanobiological interfaces, and biomaterials.

The research primarily focuses on mRNA vaccines and tumor immune mechanisms, AI-enabled RNA sequence optimization and biological functions, in vivo targeted delivery of nucleic acid molecules, in vivo cell and organ editing, and flexible biosensors.

Team Composition and Philosophy

The research team currently comprises 2 postdoctoral fellows, 8 doctoral students, 24 master's students, and 12 undergraduates. Adhering to the talent development philosophy of “pioneering frontiers, innovation-driven, open-minded, and collaborative advancement,” the team has established a systematic research training framework focused on cutting-edge biomedical directions. It is dedicated to cultivating high-level, multidisciplinary talents equipped with original thinking, an exploratory spirit, and solid scientific research competencies. The team has guided students to win a gold medal in the International Genetically Engineered Machine Competition (iGEM) and a second prize in the Liaoning Province College Student Life Sciences Innovation and Entrepreneurship Competition. Multiple graduate students have received prestigious honors such as the National Scholarship for Master's Students and the Dalian University of Technology Doctoral Excellence Scholarship, demonstrating the team's continuous improvement in talent development quality.
