Admissions and Recruitment Information

Join Our Research Team

We are actively seeking passionate and talented individuals to join our innovative research team. If you are driven by curiosity, committed to scientific discovery, and eager to contribute to cutting-edge research at the intersection of mRNA technology and bio-interface science, we invite you to explore opportunities in our dynamic laboratory.

Why Join Our Lab?

  • Innovative Research: Work on frontier projects that bridge mRNA therapeutics, nanomedicine, and bio-interface technologies
  • Collaborative Environment: Join a diverse team of passionate researchers in a supportive, interdisciplinary setting
  • State-of-the-Art Facilities: Access cutting-edge laboratory equipment and computational resources
  • Mentorship & Development: Receive personalized guidance and opportunities for professional growth
  • Impactful Research: Contribute to scientific advancements with potential real-world applications in medicine and biotechnology

We welcome applications from master's students, doctoral candidates, postdoctoral researchers, and visiting scholars with backgrounds in molecular biology, bioengineering, pharmaceutical sciences, computational biology, materials science, or related fields.
