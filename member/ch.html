Personal Profile

Cheng Hao, Ph.D., is a postdoctoral researcher at the institution, having graduated from China Pharmaceutical University. His research focuses on gene drug design, transfection, and delivery. In recent years, he has led six research projects in this field, including the National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC) Young Scientist Fund Project, the Provincial Natural Science Foundation Young Scientist Fund Project, and the China Postdoctoral Science Foundation General Project. He has published 19 SCI papers as first/corresponding author in pharmaceutical-related journals such as Advanced Science, Journal of Chemical Engineering, Nano-Micro Letters, Nano Letters, and Biomaterials. He also holds 9 papers with an Impact Factor (IF) >10. He has published 19 SCI papers as first/corresponding author in pharmaceutical journals including Adv Sci, Chem Eng J, Nano-Micro Lett, Nano Lett, and Biomaterials (9 with IF > 10). He also serves as a young editorial board member for the Chinese Journal of Pharmaceutical Industry and the Journal of Shenyang Pharmaceutical University.
