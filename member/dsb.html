Personal Profile

Du Shubo, Associate Professor and Master's Supervisor. He obtained his Ph.D. in Science from the National University of Singapore in 2019, followed by postdoctoral research at the Department of Chemistry, National University of Singapore. He joined Dalian University of Technology in 2022. His research focuses on biomolecular targeted controlled release, addressing key scientific challenges in novel vaccine and antibody drug development, cancer cell detection and therapy. He has conducted studies on intracellular delivery of antibody drugs, tumor suppression mechanisms, and exploration of tumor cell proteomics, yielding a series of innovative publications. His research outcomes have been published in 20 SCI-indexed papers, appearing in prestigious international journals such as ACS Central Science, Journal of the American Chemical Society, and Angewandte Chemie International Edition.
