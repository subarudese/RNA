Research

Advancing mRNA technology and bio-interface research for innovative healthcare solutions

Research Overview

The mRNA Technology and Bio-Interface Lab investigates how biologic systems interact with engineered synthetic materials, with a particular emphasis on mRNA therapeutics, nanocarrier design, and bioelectronic interfaces.

Grounded in biomaterials science, our research aims to translate molecular and interfacial insights into clinically relevant therapeutic and diagnostic technologies. Through interdisciplinary collaboration, we bridge nucleic acids, nanotechnology, and bio-synthetic interfaces to accelerate the development of clinically impactful biomedical solutions and therapies.
